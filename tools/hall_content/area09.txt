The Great Ammonite Wall | a stone wall panel studded with a dozen spiral ammonites of various sizes, set into a frame of grey rock | Grey stone wall | Opal spirals | make every ammonite shimmer opal with pink and green fire on the grey rock | Painted wall | make the ammonites painted in bright coral, cream and teal on a pale plaster ground
